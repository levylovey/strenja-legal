Privacy Policy for Strenja

Last updated: July 3, 2026

Strenja ("we", "our", "the app") is a proximity-based social app that helps you connect with nearby users. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Location data: We collect your approximate location while the app is in use to detect nearby users and enable the "wave" matching feature.
  • Profile information: Name, avatar/photo, and any profile details you choose to add.
  • Contact information: When you match with another user via a "wave," limited contact details may be shared between matched users to enable connection.
  • Device and usage data: Basic technical data (device type, app version) used for app functionality and crash diagnostics.

How We Use Your Information

  • To show you nearby users and enable wave-based matching.
  • To facilitate contact sharing between matched users.
  • To send push notifications about waves, matches, and app updates.
  • To maintain app security, including handling user reports and blocking.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Location and profile data are only shared with other users as part of the app's core matching functionality (i.e., when you mutually match with someone nearby).

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Your Choices

  • You can disable location permissions at any time via your device settings, though this will limit core app functionality.
  • You can disable push notifications via your device settings.
  • You can request account and data deletion by contacting us.
