Child Safety Standards

Last updated: July 5, 2026

Strenja is an 18-and-over platform. We do not permit minors to use the app, and we maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form.

Age Restriction

Strenja requires all users to confirm they are 18 years of age or older during account creation, verified against the date of birth provided at signup. Accounts found to belong to minors are suspended and removed.

Zero Tolerance Policy

Strenja strictly prohibits any content or behavior that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors. This includes but is not limited to imagery, messages, profile content, or any attempt to use the platform to contact, groom, or exploit a minor.

Reporting Mechanism

Strenja provides an in-app reporting feature that allows any user to report another user for inappropriate behavior, including suspected child exploitation or safety concerns. Reports are reviewed and the reported account may be suspended pending investigation.

Escalation to Authorities

Strenja complies with applicable child safety laws and will report confirmed instances of CSAE to the relevant national and international authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable.
